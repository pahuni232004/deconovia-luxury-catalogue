Objects of Singular Desire

Each Deconovia piece is conceived as a living monument — a union of rare material, architectural form, integrated illumination and living botanicals. Conceived for interiors that demand the extraordinary.

№ 01

Eclipse
Noir

Crescent Arc Botanical Tower

Six crescent-shaped forms in matte anthracite ceramic stack in alternating orientation, each inner arc concealing a warm amber LED. Between the arcs, plantings of yellow-green herb and fern nestle in shadow and light. Eclipse Noir is an object of severe elegance — conceived for those who find beauty in restraint and power in the absolute absence of excess.

Material
Anthracite Ceramic
Form
Crescent Arc Stack
Lighting
Arc-inset Amber
№ 02

Bianco
Pagoda

White Marble Tiered Tower

Four diminishing tiers of white Carrara marble with waisted profiles ascend in a pagoda silhouette, each tier illuminated from below by a hidden warm-amber band. Open niches in each level shelter fresh herb pods and ripe cherry clusters. Bianco Pagoda brings a quiet architectural grandeur to intimate interiors — the dining table, the console, the library shelf.

Material
Carrara Marble
Finish
Matte & Polished
Lighting
Under-tier Amber
№ 03

Athénée

Neoclassical Fluted Urn Column

Conceived as an homage to classical architecture, Athénée is a monumental fluted urn column in warm limestone composite. Its scalloped flutes are punctuated at intervals by planted alcoves from which berry clusters and flowering vine cascade in vivid contrast against the chalky white surface. Integrated LED bands at each register illuminate the cascades from below, evoking candlelight in a grand salon.

Material
Limestone Composite
Finish
Chalky Matte
Lighting
Tier LED
№ 04

Azur
Imperial

Grand Delft Porcelain Column

A majestic column clad in hand-painted Delft-inspired porcelain tiles, each panel a tapestry of cobalt floral and geometric motifs on ivory ground, edged in 22-carat gold. Illuminated from within, the translucent glaze radiates a celestial warmth. Cascading tomato vines and herb clouds emerge from each arched planting alcove in abundant, theatrical profusion. A piece born for grand palaces, landmark hotels and flagship interiors of international distinction.

Material
Hand-painted Porcelain
Motif
Delft Cobalt Floral
Edge
22-carat Gold

Obsidian
Totem

Geometric Cube-Sphere Tower

Cubes and spheres in matte obsidian ceramic alternate in precise vertical composition, each geometric volume perforated to hold cascading moss and fern. Illuminated shelves between each form cast light upward and downward simultaneously — creating a rhythm of shadow and glow that animates the tower from floor to crown. An exercise in architectural rigour softened by living matter.

Material
Obsidian Ceramic
Form
Cube + Sphere
Lighting
Shelf-mounted LED

Lumière

Figurative Botanical Floor Lamp

She is simultaneously floor lamp, botanical planter and prestige sculpture. No other object transforms a room with the same quiet authority. A singular commission for the most discerning of collections.
